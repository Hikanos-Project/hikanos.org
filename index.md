# Gain financial clarity for your peace of mind & give surplus for purpose.

A free cohort program for post-exit founders.

[Apply for next cohort](https://tally.so/r/pbjkqJ)

## Where you are as a post-exit founder

### Outside

- A level of success few ever reach and few can relate to
- No obvious peer group for the questions that come next
- **Financial independence reached, $5m–$50m in liquid wealth**

### Inside

- A sudden change in wealth level and a different role, or no role at all
- Feelings of uneasiness, restlessness, a sense of separation from other people
- **Establishing a new identity for yourself & in relation to others**

## Why Hikanos?

Combining a meaningful quantity of "enough" and the feeling of having arrived.

Ancient Greek for: sufficient, considerable, much
Also: appropriate, competent, worthy
From the root verb *hiko*: to arrive

## What you can gain

1. **Share notes with peers who have been there**: The cohort has had similar experiences, so you can speak openly
2. **Individual financial planning without perverse incentives**: Clarity that brings peace of mind
3. **A purpose for the surplus**: Strategic giving as one part of a new mission

## How the program works

- **6 online sessions**: 2 hours each, bi-weekly cadence, 4 group + 2 individual sessions
- **3 months**: Working through having enough, feeling enough, doing enough
- **8 founders at most**: One committed cohort, the same people throughout

**Completely free.** If it helped, recommend us to your friends & optionally pay it forward for future participants.

### Core elements of our exploration

- **HAVING enough**: Financial clarity, security, confidence
- **FEELING enough**: Satisfaction, Belonging, Peace of mind
- **DOING enough**: Purpose, Meaning, Impact

### Program outline

| Month | Session | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Group Session 1 | Building peer connections and trust, overview of program & personal stories with money, identity, success, and which of these to pass on |
| 01 | 1-on-1 Session | Collecting personal input & assumptions with your Hikanos Planner |
| 02 | Group Session 2 | Examining different concepts to pass on wealth & how you feel about your spending |
| 02 | 1-on-1 Session | Developing your own needs planning & modeling your surplus |
| 03 | Group Session 3 | All share their numbers and the processes behind them & explore mixed feelings about contributing wealth to the world |
| 03 | Group Session 4 | Developing your "Surplus for Purpose" action plan & closing with next steps for each cohort member |
| Yearly | Reunion call | Sharing experiences what worked and what did not & celebrate your giving |

## What you take away

1. **Your personal "enough" level**: A framework you have worked through and can trust to be sufficient
2. **Your financial plan**: For spending, gifting & inheritance, as well as any future income
3. **Your plan for the surplus**: For your next mission, identity, and strategic giving

**Full confidentiality**: Everything stays within your group, and we never share your data with any financial advisors, etc.

## The "Surplus for Purpose" ecosystem

Hikanos is one of four programs, each for a different wealth situation.

| Program | For | How |
|---|---|---|
| [Original Position](https://original-position.org) | High earners building surplus from income | Free app-based program, optional paid coaching |
| [Enough Project](https://www.enough-project.org) | Families, inheritors, entrepreneurs with $100m+ | Free cohorts with 1-on-1 financial planning |
| [Metron Advisory](https://metronadvisory.org) | Inheritors with $10m–$100m wealth | Paid 1-on-1 advisory on giving & impact investing |
